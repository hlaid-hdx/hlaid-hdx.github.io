A playful field guide to understanding crows, their habits, and their extremely serious relationship with nuts.

What are they saying?

Crows are intelligent, observant, and surprisingly expressive animals. This page imagines a world where crow messages can be understood by studying the objects they carry. In this completely serious research project, nuts are treated as one of the most important clues in crow communication.

The Nuts of Conversation

While most people think crows only communicate with calls, Crow Speak asks a more important question: what if the real message is in the nut?

Walnut

A walnut may mean the crow is calm, observant, and willing to negotiate with nearby humans. Approach with respect.

Acorn

An acorn may signal curiosity. The crow could be watching you, judging you, or preparing to make an important announcement.

Peanut

A peanut may indicate affection, trust, or an attempt to begin a very small business transaction.

Respect the local crow population.

Crows and other black birds are often treated as spooky symbols, but they are intelligent animals that deserve respect. Watch them from a distance, pay attention to what they carry, and avoid feeding them foods that would not naturally be part of their diet.

Respect

Give crows space, especially near nests, food sources, or groups of birds.

Observe

Notice where they land, what they carry, and how they react to their surroundings.

Protect

Avoid leaving trash or processed food where wildlife might eat it.
